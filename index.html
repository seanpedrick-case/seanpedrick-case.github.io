About

I'm a data scientist working in local government in London. My work sits at the intersection of public services and AI in a resource-constrained environment. In my time in my current role I have created numerous data science tools to help improve services in the borough. These include an app to analyse open-text survey data with LLMs, a tool to link free text address data, and an app to make sensitive documents safe to share through redaction.

For all of these solutions, safety, and reliability are paramount to best serve our residents. For this reason, I have a particular interest in creating highly secure AI solutions with evaluation and monitoring at the forefront of design.

I work on other projects that I can't yet share publicly as a repo, but I have also done a lot of work to create machine learning models within a social care setting to improve the efficiency of repetitive processes in the review process. More details on my other projects can be seen on my LinkedIn profile.
